TLDR: [Play Hex: https://playhex.org](https://playhex.org)

I developed a platform to play Hex online, against an other player, or vs an AI.

The platform is open source (APGL license). I first wanted to try out socket.io to develop a multiplayer game.

But I like this game, and I did not found other platform where I could easily play against an AI to try the game,
or find opponent without creating an account.

So I continued the development. I added a lobby, player accounts, time control...
